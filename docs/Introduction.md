# INTRODUCTION

As we know, public transport is an important part of daily life in the Klang Valley. Many people depend on Rapid KL bus to travel to work or university. To help commuters plan their journey the Prasarana provides the MyRapid Pulse mobile app which an app that shows bus routes and live location. However, the app does not give reliable arrival times at each bus stop that lead into problem which the commuters often wait without knowing when their bus will come (Khazanah Reaserch Institute, 2025).
